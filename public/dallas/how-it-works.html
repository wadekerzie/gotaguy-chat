Straight Talk
How it works
1

We send you a job by text

You get a text with the job type, neighborhood, and a price range the homeowner has already agreed to. Reply CLAIM [job number] to take it.

2

Show up and agree on a final price

Walk the job with the homeowner and land on an exact number within the range they already agreed to. If you get on site and find the job is bigger than expected, something behind a wall, under a sink, anything you couldn't see upfront, you and the homeowner agree on a new number. That updated number is what gets charged. No surprises for either side.

3

Do the work, text DONE

When the job is complete, text DONE [job number]. The homeowner confirms and GotaGuy sends payment to your debit card fast, within 1-2 business days after the job closes. You will also receive an automatic job summary email with the trade, final price, and payment amount for your records.
